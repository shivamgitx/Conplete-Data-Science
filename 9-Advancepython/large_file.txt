Droupadi Murmu is the 15th and current President of India, having assumed office on July 25, 2022. She is the head of state, supreme commander of the Indian Armed Forces, and the first person from a tribal community to hold this constitutional position.

Key Details About President Droupadi Murmu
Term Began: July 25, 2022.
Background: Born on June 20, 1958, in Uparbeda village, Odisha, she belongs to the Santhal tribal community.
Previous Roles: Served as the 9th Governor of Jharkhand from 2015 to 2021.
Residence: Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi.

Role and Election
Role: As the Head of State, the President is the formal head of the executive, legislature, and judiciary, representing the unity and integrity of the nation.
Election: Elected by an electoral college consisting of elected members of both Houses of Parliament (Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha) and Legislative Assemblies of states and union territories.
Qualifications: A citizen of India, at least 35 years old, and not holding any office of profit.
